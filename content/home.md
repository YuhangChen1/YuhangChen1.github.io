## About

I am a final-year undergraduate in Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I am currently a research intern at Columbia University, working with Prof. Shilong Liu, and at InSpatio. My research focuses on interactive world models, spatial reasoning, and unified multimodal models for embodied AI. Previously, I worked with Prof. Tianyi Zhou at the University of Maryland and Prof. Yao Wan at ONE Lab, HUST.
