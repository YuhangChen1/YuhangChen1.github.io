Research Profile

I am a junior undergraduate in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China. My research interests lie in world models, world action models, multimodal foundation models, and AI agents. I am continuing to take my initial steps in scientific research, always as a learner.
